# Private/internal routes (/admin, /studio, /google-ads, /Desktop-view,
# /SEO-overview) are kept out of search via a `noindex, nofollow` meta tag
# in each route's head(). They are intentionally NOT Disallowed here:
# Google must be able to crawl a page to see its noindex tag. Blocking them

# URL-only ("Indexed, though blocked by robots.txt"). noindex is the correct
# and sufficient mechanism for reliable de-indexing.
