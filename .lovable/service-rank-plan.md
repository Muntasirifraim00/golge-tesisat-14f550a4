## Service-page ranking playbook (applied to every target page)
1. One primary keyword per page; title starts with it, ≤60 chars, + İstanbul + benefit (7/24, kırmadan, fiyat).
2. H1 = primary keyword variant; H2s cover sub-intents: nasıl yapılır, fiyatları, hangi cihazlar, kaç saatte, hangi ilçeler, garanti, SSS.
3. Depth beyond competitors: 1,800–2,500 words useful content, price table (TL range), process steps, device list, before/after cases.
4. Trust/E-E-A-T: real phone, address, years, warranty, reviews block, technician photos; no invented facts.
5. Schema: Service + LocalBusiness/Plumber + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList (+ Offer price range).
6. Local signals: district grid linking to /tesisatci/{ilçe}/{service}; "en yakın" phrase near district list.
7. Internal links: blogs with high impressions → service page with exact anchor; home + header + footer link; hub↔fiyat.
8. Conversion: sticky call/WhatsApp, above-fold CTA, short form — page must turn visits into calls.
9. Canonical clean (ad ?hizmet= params must not get indexed), fast LCP, mobile first.
10. Re-measure in GSC after 2–4 weeks; iterate titles where CTR < 2%.
